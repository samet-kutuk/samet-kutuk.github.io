May took me on the road for most of the month — the SOAFEE APAC Seminar in Tokyo, the Autonomous Vehicle Technology Expo in Stuttgart, the Electrified and Autonomous Systems Dive In Day in Zürich, and AutoTech Detroit.

At IEEE MOST in Newark, Boyang Tian ran an Autoware tutorial and Open AD Kit demo, Pedro Rito and João Amaral from Universidade de Aveiro showed how V2X can create a shared perception environment, and the CAR Lab at University of Delaware unveiled its Star Shuttle. At the SOAFEE APAC Seminar in Tokyo, we debuted the pre-launch video of the Open AD Kit Blueprint, and Autoware featured in over half the presentations and many of the showcase demos — a clear signal of traction. At ICRA 2025 in Atlanta, The Roboracer Foundation held its 24th autonomous racing competition, the AutoDRIVE Ecosystem ran its third Roboracer online sim racing league with 58 teams, and we met the Duckietown Robotics team for the first time. At the 16th ITS European Congress in Seville, our friends at the COLUMBUSS project showed their Autoware-powered bus, offering free autonomy software for public transport.

At the Autonomous Vehicle Tech Expo Europe in Stuttgart, we didn't manage a pavilion booth this year, but there was a strong sense of community with friends from Deepen AI, ASAM, MORAI, Autonoma, Alternative Embedded Innovation, and NIT Academy. Muhammad Zain Khawaja presented on our privately owned vehicle stack and the Open AD Kit. The state of the German automotive industry was felt at the show, and it reinforced something I keep coming back to: open source and open collaboration can be part of the remedy. At the Electrified and Autonomous Systems Dive In Day in Zürich, hosted by Switzerland Innovation Park Zurich, G. Alexander Kolbai brought his instrumented vehicle and a scaled version of it, and we discussed the opportunities Switzerland's clear regulatory framework creates, closing the day with barbecue and good conversation.

From there it was on to Detroit for AutoTech Detroit, alongside COVESA, eSync Alliance, and SOAFEE — our first time sponsoring the COVESA AutoTech Networking Event.
