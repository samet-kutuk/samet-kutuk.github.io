I visited South Korea as part of a broader trip that also took me to Singapore, spending time in three Korean cities and connecting with local players and decision-makers. Out of that trip, [TREEZE](https://www.treeze-eng.com) joined the Autoware Foundation as a Premium Member.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/MMoWJi6oMbuRvL45A)

TREEZE bridges the automotive industry, academia, research institutions, and regulatory bodies, with over a decade of hands-on experience turning conventional vehicles into autonomous platforms. As a full-stack autonomous driving solutions provider, they specialize in Level 4 autonomy for special-purpose vehicles — shuttles, yard tractors, logistics robots, research platforms, and client-provided vehicles — and are deeply rooted in the Korean ecosystem, working with provincial governments and institutions like KATECH on nationwide testbed programs.

TREEZE has already built a TRL-6+ AD stack that combines Autoware with their own proprietary modules. As a foundation member, they'll contribute to the Low Speed Autonomy Working Group and the Open AD Kit Working Group, focused on perception, localization, planning, and control for low-speed environments, sharing real-world deployment data from their fleet, and providing test platforms to validate OpenAD Kit-based autonomy solutions.
