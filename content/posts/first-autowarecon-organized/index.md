I did all the preparation for the [first AutowareCon](https://autoware.org/a-decade-in-motion-the-first-autowarecon/), which took place at Nagoya University in Japan — where Autoware began — entirely from Zürich.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/x2n3xCKiHFpPr6jU6)

The event, held on September 10 and sponsored by NEDO (New Energy and Industrial Technology Development Organization), brought the global Autoware community together in person and online. It included keynotes and updates from Autoware Foundation leaders, a deep dive into Autoware 2.0 and its vision for end-to-end autonomous driving, continued discussion around Autoware Foundation 2.0 strategy, and tech talks and deployment stories submitted by the community.

Coordinating an in-person conference on the other side of the world, remotely, with a hybrid online audience to plan for as well, was its own exercise in logistics.
