I had a few personal updates to share about my professional journey that were long overdue. Having juggled multiple roles and organizations over the past few years, I never had a clean moment to lay out where things stood while the transitions were still uncertain. Here's where things landed.

As of November 2024, I fully stepped away from Leo Drive, the company I co-founded in 2015. I'm thankful for the experience, the learning, and the collaborations from that time — if it weren't for Leo Drive, I would never have found my way into autonomous vehicles and open-source communities in the first place.

At the Autoware Foundation, I served in various capacities over the past two years, including the Board of Directors, the Strategic Planning Committee, and the Marketing Steering Committee. I stepped down from the Board in November 2024 to take on a contract role at the foundation, and my term on the Strategic Planning Committee concluded when I completed my two-year duty. Going forward, I continue at the foundation as Head of Marketing / Community Advocate, which means continuing most of what I was already doing — managing events, owning the communication pipeline, community advocacy, and facilitating collaboration.

I also founded my own company, Open Autonomy Advisors, in Zürich, almost a year ago now. Through it, I continue my work with the Autoware Foundation as a contractor while exploring other ways to contribute to the open-source AV ecosystem.

As the father of two young daughters, and soon to be a father of a son, I've come to appreciate simplicity in life, especially in a turbulent world.
