I represented the Autoware Foundation at the final SAAM Day of the year, organized by the Swiss Association for Autonomous Mobility and hosted by Swisscom. I introduced Autoware and the foundation to the room and invited members to collaborate with open-source projects like it.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/NJq98VihpSm2pRKG7)

We heard updates from pioneering Swiss projects spanning on-demand autonomous ride-pooling, autonomous mobility services closing public transport gaps, and private-site autonomous shuttle operations. Hearing directly from the teams building these things was energizing.

The message I emphasized was that open source has always been the substrate for mass adoption, and for something as complex as autonomous driving, collaboration isn't optional — it's essential. That's the whole premise behind Autoware: open technology, open governance, open collaboration across the ecosystem. From what I've seen over the past year, a number of international stakeholders are looking to Switzerland as a model — Swiss pragmatism, engineering excellence, and structured deployment approaches could shape adoption across Europe.

I also heard about the upcoming Swiss roadmap for autonomous vehicles and the next SAAM Summit, and we're already in touch with the SAAM office about how to contribute to both.
