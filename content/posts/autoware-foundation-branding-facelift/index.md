After redesigning the Autoware Foundation website when I started with the organization, I gave the branding another update to bring messaging and outreach activities to a consistent look and feel. The refreshed [Brand Guideline](https://autoware.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Autoware-Foundation_Brand-Guideline.pdf) is now live.

As Autoware grows — more members, more deployments, more research, more events — the way we present ourselves needs to grow with it and stay consistent. The update includes cleaner and more modular page layouts, updated presentation templates for talks, meetings, and events, enhanced icon and background kits for consistent storytelling, and clearer guidance for social media visuals, documents, and community resources.

Autoware is a community effort, and the identity representing it should be built the same way — with room for feedback to shape the next iterations.
