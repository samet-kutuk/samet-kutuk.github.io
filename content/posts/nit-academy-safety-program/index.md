The joint safety training program with NIT Academy that I helped start almost single-handedly moved into its cohort project stage. It began at CES 2024, when Mohammad Mosa, Milan Bjelica, Simon Thompson, and I sat down and worked out the idea together. NIT Academy later became a member of the Autoware Foundation and, in return, opened this program to a cohort of Autoware community members.

Participants came together in a regular online session to present their first project work, applying what they'd learned on functional safety fundamentals. Topics covered so far included the automotive standards landscape, an ISO 26262 overview, operational modes and situations, hazard analysis and risk assessment (HARA), and ASIL decomposition.

I was a student in the program myself, but had to forfeit my place partway through when my priorities shifted and my contract wasn't extended. Even so, it's been good to watch the cohort progress — both the instructors and the Safety Assurance Working Group have been pleased with the engagement and the quality of the questions. Next up after the holiday break is SOTIF and a new set of modules going deeper into real-world safety practices.
