Jérémy Cohen from [Think Autonomous](https://www.thinkautonomous.ai/), whom I'd known for two years before this, interviewed me about Autoware and open-source autonomous vehicle development.

{{< youtube _0oF49oBjcE >}}

We talked about Autoware — from modular robotics to End-to-End learning, and how open collaboration continues to shape the future of autonomy. Since the interview, we've seen a real surge of new followers and discussions across the community. Thanks to Jeremy and the Think Autonomous team for helping bring Autoware's story to new audiences.

Our teams are also working on making Autoware easier to learn — building a more comprehensive training path for people to master the platform. It's a big system with a lot of layers, but every minute spent learning it pays off in real-world capability.
