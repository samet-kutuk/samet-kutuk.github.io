I initiated the bilateral membership between [ASAM e.V. and the Autoware Foundation](https://autoware.org/asam-and-the-autoware-foundation-are-partnering-to-advance-autonomous-driving/) — ASAM joined as an Alliance Partner of the foundation, and the foundation joined ASAM as a member in return.

Autoware already leverages ASAM's OpenX standards, including OpenSCENARIO and OpenDRIVE, to power its simulation workflows. We're now evaluating ASAM SOVD for the Open AD Kit Blueprint, to explore its potential for accessing and managing software-defined vehicles. The collaboration was catalyzed by Deepen AI, an active member of both communities, through a series of meetups bringing together ASAM, Autoware, and the broader ecosystem.

Christian John, board member at the Autoware Foundation, and Marius Dupuis, CEO of ASAM, both spoke to the shared premise: that standardization and open collaboration aren't at odds with open source, and that aligning efforts helps broaden awareness and adoption of both.
