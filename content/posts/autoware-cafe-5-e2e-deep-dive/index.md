The fifth episode of the Autoware Café was a deep dive into Autoware's End-to-End autonomous driving journey, guided by our own open, safe, and modular philosophy.

{{< youtube SBAqIvHSFrc >}}

End-to-end autonomous driving is fast becoming the next major evolution in autonomy, with innovators like Wayve and NVIDIA advancing data-driven, AI-first approaches. The Autoware community had expressed a clear intention to move in that direction, and our 56-page E2E report — already downloaded hundreds of times and one of the most requested topics in an earlier Autoware Café poll — laid out how.

I hosted Muhammad Zain Khawaja, Managing Director of Product at the Autoware Foundation, to walk through the four evolutionary steps from Learned Planning to Learned Hybrid, the Generator-Selector framework that balances AI innovation with safety assurance, how simulation, data, and MLOps are reshaping model development, and why explainability and Safety Guardians matter for reaching Level 4+ autonomy.
