I went to Indianapolis to claim the free paddock space given to the Autoware Foundation as part of the joint sim racing activity, and to put several Autoware-powered vehicle platforms on display. I pulled this off on my own, in a city I'd never been to before. I also joined a panel on simulation at the Indy Autonomous Challenge event.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/8BepeVLHB584YE3p6)

We set up at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway for the Indy Autonomous Challenge race, with autonomous racecars competing in the early afternoon and the Autoware Foundation showing at paddocks 28 and 29.
