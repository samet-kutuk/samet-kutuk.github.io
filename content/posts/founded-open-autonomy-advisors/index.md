## The Beginning

In 2024 I established **Open Autonomy Advisors GmbH** in Zürich — my vehicle for fractional advisory, ecosystem strategy, and business development work across the autonomous vehicle and robotics industry.

This wasn't a spontaneous decision. It was the result of nearly a decade of hands-on work in the AV industry across Turkey, Japan, and Switzerland, and a growing conviction that the ecosystem needed a different kind of advisor — one who had actually built things, not just observed them.

---

## What the Firm Does

Open Autonomy Advisors works with a small number of clients at any given time. The focus areas are:

- **Technology evaluation** — helping companies understand what they're actually buying or building
- **Ecosystem strategy** — who to partner with, who to watch, where the leverage is
- **Business development** — opening doors in European and international markets
- **Regulatory navigation** — particularly in the Swiss and EU context

> "The autonomous vehicle industry doesn't need more consultants who learned the terminology last year. It needs people who have been in the room when things went wrong — and know why."

---

## Why Zürich

Switzerland was a deliberate choice. The density of serious technical work happening here — at ETH, at the Swiss Transit Lab, across the Innosuisse-funded project landscape — is genuinely rare in Europe.

Zürich also sits at a useful intersection: close enough to German engineering culture, connected to the broader European regulatory environment, and internationally minded enough to work across borders without friction.

---

## The Fractional Model

The fractional engagement model is intentional. It means:

| What it means | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Small number of clients | Full attention, not divided focus |
| Defined scope | Clear deliverables, not open-ended retainers |
| Senior involvement only | You work with me, not a team I supervise |
| Flexible duration | Engagements that match the actual need |

---

## A Note on Open Source

One of the non-negotiables for me is maintaining a genuine connection to the open source AV ecosystem — particularly Autoware. Advisory work that drifts too far from the actual technology stops being useful quickly.

> Open source isn't charity. It's infrastructure. And in autonomous driving, it may be the only realistic path to the safety validation scale the industry actually needs.
