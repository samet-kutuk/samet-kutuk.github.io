I've been championing this for a long time, and it's finally live: the [Open AD Kit Blueprint](https://www.soafee.io/blog/2025/openadkit-blueprint/), the first-ever SOAFEE Blueprint, is now on the SOAFEE website with the video I created and the blog I wrote.

{{< youtube kyKtTM1sc2o >}}

The Autoware Foundation developed the blueprint in close collaboration with SOAFEE, the eSync Alliance, and a wide network of contributors across the SDV and autonomous driving ecosystems, including ADLINK Technology, Ampere, Arm, AWS, Corellium, Excelfore, MulticoreWare, PIX Moving, Red Hat, TIER IV, ZettaScale Technology, and 7STARLAKE. It demonstrates how autonomous-driving software can be developed cloud-natively, using modular, containerized workloads that can be tested rapidly, updated continuously, and deployed across heterogeneous compute platforms, from cloud to vehicle ECUs.

The blueprint has already been adopted and showcased by automotive and technology players including Arm, AWS, Corellium, DENSO, Red Hat, and Synopsys, each extending and validating the architecture through their own demos, learning paths, and integrations. Next up is work on operating mixed-critical applications on the Open AD Kit, along with enhancements through AI-powered, containerized autonomy workloads — more of that at CES 2026.
