I organized the CES booth party at the TIER IV booth again this year, on top of an otherwise packed agenda talking to prospective and existing Autoware Foundation members.

The lineup for Las Vegas included the Connected Vehicle Systems Alliance (COVESA) Showcase Event at the Bellagio Hotel on January 7th, the CES 2025 Open AD Kit Meetup at the TIER IV booth on January 8th, and the Indy Autonomous Challenge Race at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway on January 9th. Many Autoware Foundation members were at CES this year, some with their own booths and others joining us at the social event — AMD, Arm, AUTOCRYPT, AWS, Beyless, Capgemini, Deepen AI, Hesai Technology, Indy Autonomous Challenge, Macnica, Marvell Technology, Red Hat, RoboSense, TomTom, and VicOne among them.

Over 70 automotive professionals had already signed up for the meetup at the TIER IV booth ahead of the show.
