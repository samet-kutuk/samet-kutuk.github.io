The second episode of the Autoware Café looked at what it takes to put an autonomous vehicle through the German driver's license test.

{{< youtube nlNLIU7bTjM >}}

I hosted Ilir Tahiraj and Dominik Kulmer from the Technical University of Munich to talk about EDGAR, their autonomous vehicle, which had previously wowed crowds at Oktoberfest and was now aiming to earn a real license ahead of IAA Mobility in Munich. We'd covered EDGAR before and even extended an invitation to other AV teams to compete, but this time it was a chance to hear directly from the people building it about their strategy. We also talked about TUM's exclusive use of Autoware as their go-to research and development framework, and about the state of autonomous driving in Germany from the perspective of a university that's a talent hub for the country's automotive industry.
