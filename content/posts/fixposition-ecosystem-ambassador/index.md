I started collaborating with [Fixposition](https://www.fixposition.com/) as an Ecosystem Ambassador, on top of my existing engagements.

This one goes back a while. I first worked with Fixposition in 2019 when I moved to Zürich. Later, at the Autoware Foundation, I brought them in as a member — Zhenzhong Su and the team provided Vision-RTK 2 sensors to the Autoware CoE network. One of those units ended up on Rahul Mangharam's autonomous go-kart at the University of Pennsylvania, which went on to win the Autonomous Karting Series Grand Prix at Purdue University. The Vision-RTK 2 was largely credited as the sensor that brought home the win.

Fixposition builds precise global positioning for the places GNSS fails: urban canyons, tunnels, under tree canopies. The Vision-RTK 2 fuses RTK-GNSS, computer vision, IMU, and wheel odometry to deliver centimeter-level accuracy in real time, and it's already trusted by 150+ OEMs across dev kits, software licensing, SoMs, and tailored design-win engagements. Teams using ROS and Nav2 get support for it out of the box.

In this role I'll be at events and roadshows, and I'm especially interested in proof-of-value activities with communities where Fixposition and I can show up with actual manpower rather than just slides.
