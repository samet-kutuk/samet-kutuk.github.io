I started the Autoware Café, a new podcast series bringing conversations with people from the Autoware community.

{{< youtube Guhl6wCxe8Y >}}

For the first episode, I sat down with Muhammad Zain Khawaja, Managing Director of Product at the Autoware Foundation, who walked through the Privately Owned Vehicle initiative — a greenfield project delivering an AI-first, open-source autonomous driving stack for OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers, spanning L2+, L2++, L3, and eventually L4, all built on AI foundation models. We covered the journey of building open-source AI models like SceneSeg, Scene3D, EgoPath, and EgoLanes, a high-level look at the product roadmap — how sensors, capabilities, and features would layer in over time — and the go-to-market strategy and short-term plays already in motion.
