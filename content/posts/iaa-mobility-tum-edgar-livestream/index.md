I took an autonomous ride with the TUM EDGAR vehicle at IAA Mobility in Munich and livestreamed it to AutowareCon, happening simultaneously at Nagoya University in Japan.

{{< youtube id="-ekzcaOAL7Q" start="15348" >}}

Autoware Foundation members were active throughout the show — showcasing technologies, speaking on panels, meeting with partners across the venue, with AISIN, AWS, Auve Tech, AUTOCRYPT, Capgemini Engineering, Clemson University, Hesai Technology, the FTM Institute of Automotive Technology at TUM, RoboSense, and PIX Moving all in the mix. The TUM EDGAR team offered 20-minute autonomous rides through Munich; all ride slots were fully booked, but they were still available to talk to between Halls A2 and A3.
