I organized a meetup at the Arm offices in San Jose — RSVPs, agenda, food and drinks, program, and funding were all on me. It was quite impromptu, but we got a good turnout from automotive and robotics professionals and enthusiasts.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/NY7UvvvUzFcC2FUq9)

We shared the latest updates from the Autoware Foundation and Autoware software, gave a quick overview of the Open AD Kit work, and enjoyed pizza and cold drinks courtesy of our hosts at Arm. Thanks to everyone who joined, and to the Arm team for hosting.
