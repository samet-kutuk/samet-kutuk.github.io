I organized and hosted an after-party right after ROSCon Singapore ended, at Blu Jazz on Haji Lane. About 80 people showed up for a panel, food, drinks, and conversation. I put this together from Zürich while coordinating with people in Kuala Lumpur, which made for an interesting organizational exercise.

[📷 View photo album on Google Photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/mFTUYU4kkSbhQCeA6)

The venue buzzed with developers, researchers, founders, and VCs. Sponsors were AMD and TIER IV, with community partners Mole, Open Robotics, Antler, The Runway Ventures, and Build Club helping bring it together.

https://mole.is/automaton-2025

I moderated a panel with Simon George (AMD), Simon Thompson (TIER IV), Geoffrey Biggs (Open Robotics), and Eugene Teo (Antler), covering what drives organizations to embrace open-source culture, how AI is reshaping opportunities and challenges for open-source communities, how companies measure ROI from contributing to open projects, and why open-source initiatives need to find their way into real-world products rather than staying academic.

Many thanks to Muhammad Haziq Faris Hasnol, Melly Ling, Tengku Wazir Aziz, and the rest of the Mole team, without whom this wouldn't have come together, and to Ryan Thoo and Admond Lee for helping promote it.
