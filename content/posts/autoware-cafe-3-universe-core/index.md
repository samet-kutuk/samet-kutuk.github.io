The third episode of the Autoware Café was picked by the community — after the previous episode, we asked what people wanted next, and this topic won.

{{< youtube iulZ7Jb2B2k >}}

I hosted Ryohsuke Mitsudome, TSC Chair of Autoware, to unpack the two sides of the project: Autoware Universe, the rich collection of features, capabilities, and modules for diverse autonomous driving use cases, and Autoware Core, the foundation that ensures high-quality code, robust architecture, and modern software development practices. We talked through how the two differ and work together, why Autoware Core was created and what its second phase of development brings, how shifts in architectural paradigms might shape the project's future, and why this two-legged approach matters for developers, deployers, and the ecosystem as a whole.
