Open Autonomy Advisors has joined the [Swiss Association for Autonomous Mobility (SAAM)](https://www.saam.swiss/members/) as a member. The membership came after I volunteered as AV Showcasing Lead at the Automated Mobility Summit, which SAAM organized together with PAVE Europe.

{{< youtube WIgs9ngpVzM >}}

When I moved to Zürich in 2019, I was surprised there weren't more autonomous vehicle companies here, given how dense the engineering talent is in Switzerland. For a long time the AV ecosystem felt quiet compared to the US or Asia. That's changed — WeRide, Baidu, ADASTEC, LOXO, and others have brought real pilots to Zürich and the rest of the country, and the conversations have gotten more interesting.

I started looking for a community that could help accelerate that momentum, which is how I found SAAM. I attended a SAAM Day as a guest and pitched the idea of Autoware joining forces with the association. It didn't go quite as I'd hoped at the time, but I left with something useful: word of a summit they were planning. When SAAM later invited me to volunteer as the AV Showcasing Lead at the Automated Mobility Summit, I said yes without hesitation.

I joined SAAM because I'm a community person — I think the best outcomes come from people sharing openly: knowledge, code, experience. My goal within SAAM is to promote the open-source spirit and, over time, help more people understand how the AV stack actually works, not just observe it from the outside.
