I helped Nomadic AI showcase what they're building at the TechAD conference in Berlin. I've been coming to TechAD for years, but this was the first time I attended representing an organization with a product the industry is actually looking for.

Across many editions of this show, I've noticed the same pattern in data annotation and labeling: smarter workflows, better tooling, but always an army of manual annotators behind the curtain. The unit economics of that model don't scale, and everyone in the industry knows it.

What's different at NomadicML is that manual labor isn't the foundation — the AI does the heavy lifting. This was the company's European debut, with Zoox, Bedrock Robotics, and Zendar already using the platform. More appearances will follow across autonomous driving, robotics, and construction, but only where it matters — this isn't a team that shows up everywhere.
