The [Autoware Foundation LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-autoware-foundation/) reached 10,000 followers. When I took over the account, it had slightly under 2,000.

It wasn't long ago that we were at around 5,000 followers, engaging with the community to learn what people were doing with Autoware and consistently pushing that news out. That's largely what got us to 10,000 — engineers, researchers, students, and industry partners engaging through likes, comments, and shares.
